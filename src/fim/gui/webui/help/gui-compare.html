Compare runs and sweeps

Comparison reads saved results. Nothing is rerun, and nothing is saved: reopening a comparison recomputes it.

Compare runs

  1. Choose Compare in the rail.
  2. Select two or more completed runs from the list (Run, Ended, Outcome), then choose Compare.

The screen shows two overlays:

  • Small multiples: one final-state scatter panel per run, deme 1 against deme 2. Panels name the parameters that differ between runs.
  • Trajectory overlay: one statistic over generations, one color per run. Statistic selects D, GST, EST, KST, HS, HT, or HST; switching redraws without another computation.

Runs with different seeds, configurations, or retention are compared as saved. Check that differences in N, ploidy, or migration convention are intended before you interpret a difference; see implementation conventions.

Compare sweeps

Start on Home, in either of two ways:

  • Compare sweeps… on an Experiment's row compares the sweep Studies inside it.
  • Select, tick sweep Studies from any Experiments, then choose Compare selected sweeps….

The comparison screen draws one curve per sweep, each with a distinct color and marker, over the axis you pick. Other shared axes are held at a value. It has the error-bar, scale, and theory controls of the sweep results screen; each theory curve uses its own sweep's settings. Degraded and unreliable points stay flagged.

With exactly two sweeps, a second panel shows their difference (first minus second) with a 95% Welch interval and a zero line. That interval assumes the sweeps' replicates are independent. Two sweeps built from the same seed and grid share seeds point for point; the command-line fim sweep compare reports whether seeds were different, shared, or partly shared, and does not adjust for sharing.

Sweeps can be compared only on axes they all vary, at values they share, and with the same ploidy. Otherwise the comparison is refused with the reason. See sweep a parameter for fim sweep compare.
