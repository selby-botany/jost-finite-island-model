Explore

Explore evaluates closed-form predictions from N, d, m, and μ without running a simulation. Use it to locate a regime, check a timescale, or choose sweep points before you spend computation.

Inputs and units

  • N (individuals/deme) is in individuals. Explore applies the ploidy of the current configuration to obtain gene copies.
  • d (demes), m (migration rate), and μ (mutation rate) use the scalar island-model conventions; see migration.
  • A field commits when you leave it or press Enter. Predictions then update. Explore never changes Configure until you choose Run this for real or Sweep this for real.

Predictions

The table groups predictions under Statistics, Equilibrium diversity, and Identity and regime. It covers differentiation measures, diversity, entropy, effective allele counts, identity-recovery timescales, and whether mutation is negligible. Definitions are in the statistics reference, including identity recovery and mutation-negligible equilibrium.

Predictions that do not depend on a parameter are flat along it. For example, analytic D is independent of N, and identity-recovery metrics are independent of μ.

Curves and surfaces

  • View selects Curve: one parameter or Surface: two parameters. A surface uses Rows and Columns as its two axes.
  • Select a statistic row to chart it over the chosen parameter range. Charts group compatible units (proportions, nats, allele counts, generations).
  • The slider evaluates predictions along the range without changing the input fields.

From prediction to simulation

  • Run this for real copies the four inputs to Configure, selects a new Study, and starts from your Settings.
  • Choose a sweep… picks sweep points for the displayed parameters: an interval, a count, and a spacing. The spacing is the axis scale (linear or log) or even steps of a predicted response chosen in Place points by the response of (D, GST, EST, HS, or HT). Response spacing crowds points where the prediction changes fastest, such as the migration threshold. The exact points and the planned sweep size are shown. Removing a point switches to an explicit list.
  • Sweep this for real copies the inputs to Configure, ticks Sweep, and stores the axes. See sweeps.

Response spacing produces an explicit list of values, so the sweep stays reproducible without the closed form.

Limits

  • Predictions assume equilibrium and the scalar finite island model. They do not represent topologies, matrices, unequal sizes, stochastic migrant sampling, or finite alleles.
  • Typical-deme entropy is approximate, especially at d=2.
  • Explore makes no claim about how long a run takes to reach the prediction. Use the derived burn-in shown before a run, and see convergence.
  • For programmatic access, see the Python statistics API.
